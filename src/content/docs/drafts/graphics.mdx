All four draw the same eight steps from [From idea to commit](/agentic-coding-template-docs/start/walkthrough/)
and use the same three colors (orange: you, blue: the assistant, green: a worker). They are hand-written SVG in
`src/assets/diagrams/` and take their colors from the active Starlight theme.

## a - two rows with a return arrow

Boxes with a short caption, a number per step, the BLOCK loop from reviewer back to builder, a dotted band for the
board. The most explicit variant and the one the walkthrough uses now; it has a German copy,
`idea-path-a.de.svg`.

<Diagram name="idea-path-a" />

## b - vertical track

A line with eight stations, a description for every step, a role tag at the right, a dotted bar for the board along
the whole path. Reads like a timeline, works best on a narrow screen, longest of the four.

<Diagram name="idea-path-b" />

## c - swim lanes

Three lanes (you, assistant, workers) and one numbered dot per step; the path zigzags between the lanes and shows
at a glance who does what. Least text, no BLOCK loop.

<Diagram name="idea-path-c" />

## d - loop around the board

The eight steps form a ring around "Board and journal". It stresses that every step leaves a file, but it hides
the order a little: the start at the top is only visible from the numbers.

<Diagram name="idea-path-d" />

## After you chose

- Delete the SVG files of the variants you do not want, and this page together with `de/drafts/graphics.mdx`.
- To use another variant in the walkthrough, change `name="idea-path-a"` in `start/walkthrough.mdx`. The German
  page needs its own copy of the drawing with German labels (`idea-path-<x>.de.svg`, copy `idea-path-a.de.svg`).
- Then run `npm run check:translations`, since the English walkthrough changed.
