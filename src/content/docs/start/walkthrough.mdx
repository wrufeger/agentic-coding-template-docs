This page follows one small change through the template, step by step, the way it ran in a demo project: a
survey app for a room full of people, where a voting round can have a time limit. Names, ids, dates and numbers
are invented; the files, commands and rules are the real ones. Nothing here is needed to use the template. It
only shows how the parts fit together.

The sentence behind every step: **nothing is built before you decide, and nothing counts as done before it is proven.**

<Diagram name="idea-path-a" caption="The path of an idea. Orange steps are yours, blue ones the assistant's (the orchestrator), green ones a worker's. The board and the journal record every step." />

Which steps a change needs at all, and which it may skip, is in [Concept, test, review: what needs what](/agentic-coding-template-docs/guides/what-needs-what/).

## 1. The idea

You type one line into the chat, no setup, no ceremony:

```text
idea: Show the remaining time of a voting round on the phone and on the dashboard.
```

A message that begins with `idea:`, `todo:` or `note:` is filed by a hook without a model turn, so it costs no
tokens and does not interrupt anything that is running. An `idea:` becomes a new section in your own ideas file,
`docs/ai/concept/ideas-alex.md`, word for word. The next session start notices it and has it processed. Details:
[Work entries](/agentic-coding-template-docs/concepts/work-entries/#ideas-file).

## 2. The concept with options

The assistant does not start coding. `R-work-idea-first` says an idea first gets a short concept with options and
a decision, and only then gets built. So it runs the `act-idea` skill: an `explorer` checks what exists (rounds
already have a time limit, nothing shows it), the concept goes into `docs/project/concepts/round-countdown.md`
with options, a recommendation, how it could fail and the way back. Nothing is filed and nothing is built yet: the
next step puts the decision to you.

## 3. The question in the inbox

The decision lands in the inbox as one file per question, `docs/ai/inbox/Q9-...md`:

```markdown
---
id: Q9
kind: question
for: all
status: open
created: 2026-03-12T09:41:07
---

# How should the remaining time of a round be shown?

The round's time limit already exists on the server; only the display is missing. The concept page with the reasons: `docs/project/concepts/round-countdown.md`.

- **a)** **Recommended:** A number counting down every second on phone and dashboard, from the server's clock - about
  20 min. Nothing to explain, the same on every device.

- **b)** A shrinking bar without numbers - about 25 min; harder to read at the back of a room.

- **c)** Do nothing for now - nothing changes, rounds just end.

**Answer:**
```

The board now lists it under "Waiting for you", and the status line shows `act · Q9`. That is the whole interface
to you: everything that waits on a person is in the inbox, and questions come with a recommendation, an effort and
a way out.

```text
## Waiting for you
- Q9 — How should the remaining time of a round be shown?
```

## 4. Your answer

You write below the `**Answer:**` label, in the file or by telling the chat, as short as you like:

```markdown
**Answer:** a, and it must keep counting after the phone was asleep.
```

The assistant reads the file again before booking, records the decision in the project's decisions
(`docs/project/decisions.md`), marks the question `answered`, then `done`, and takes your extra condition along
into the task. A question that is not answered stays open; a task that depends on it does not start on a guess.

## 5. The task

With the decision in hand, the assistant files it (`entries.py new ...`): your idea moves, word for word, into a
backlog item, say `B21`, linked to the concept page, and a task, `T14`, for what starts now, with a goal, check
criteria and a size as duration and tokens, never person-days. If the decision rests on an assumption worth checking
later, an ADR in the project's decisions comes with it.

```markdown
# B21: remaining time of a round on phone and dashboard (Q9 a)

**Goal:** both views show the seconds left of the current round, counted down from the server's clock.
**Done when:** a test shows the displayed value follows the server time after a 30 s pause; the existing
tests stay green.
Size: ~20 min · ~300k tkn fresh · ~6M tkn cache (1 builder, 1 reviewer, 1 test run).
```

Whenever it works, it records its state in one line (`entries.py state T14 ...`) so a fresh session, or you after
a coffee, can pick up from there. The board shows the task as running:

```text
## Open tasks
- T14 B21: remaining time of a round on phone and dashboard (running) — State 2026-03-12 10:05: builder done, review next
```

## 6. builder and reviewer

The assistant does not build the change itself. It hands a bounded assignment to a `builder`, a worker with
its own model tier, a cap on tool calls, a write scope and, because it runs tests, the tree they run in (`R-cost-delegate`, [Roles](/agentic-coding-template-docs/concepts/roles/)):

```text
Task T14, entry B21: show the remaining time of a round on phone and dashboard.
Tier: standard · Estimate: ~20 min · ~300k tkn fresh · ~6M tkn cache · Cap: 40
Write scope: app/static/**, app/rounds.py, tests/test_rounds.py
Tests: tests/test_rounds.py
```

A worker never commits and never writes to `docs/ai/`. It returns a result with evidence, at most 40 lines,
every assumption marked:

```text
Changed: app/rounds.py:41-58 (remaining_seconds), app/static/round.js:12-34, tests/test_rounds.py:+3 tests
Evidence: pytest -q tests/test_rounds.py -> 9 passed in 0.4 s (3 new)
Assumption: the server clock is the reference, not the phone's.
```

`R-work-evidence` is the reason for that last block: "done" holds only with a test run, a log, a screenshot, a
commit hash or an outside call. An unbacked result is "not verified", not "done". Then the `reviewer` (higher tier,
adversarial) looks once per task at the diff, lists its findings by severity and ends with a verdict: `ALLOW`, `CONDITIONAL` or `BLOCK`. It blocks only for an error on a path ordinary work reaches, a missing part of the assignment or a security hole; a case built only to break the change goes to the backlog with its input.
Here it says `CONDITIONAL`: the countdown reads right in the tests, but only a person can check it on a real phone
after the screen was locked. The assistant therefore files a todo `U4` for you, and names it in the commit.

## 7. The commit

Only the orchestrator commits (`R-code-commit`), and without being asked. The `act-commit` skill checks that the
evidence is in, runs the project's lint and the full test suite once on the accepted state (builder and reviewer ran only
the tests their `Tests:` line named), closes the finished task and question (they move into the archive under
`docs/ai/work/archive/`, nothing leaves `docs/` before it stands in its monthly file, and their ids stay reserved), stages by pathspec (never `git add -A`) and writes the journal entry. The message follows the project's
commit format (`commit-format` in [Formats](/agentic-coding-template-docs/reference/configuration/#formats); the
default `auto` keeps the repo's own style), and with the default changelog setting it also adds a line under
`[Unreleased]` in `CHANGELOG.md`, since this commit has a visible effect:

```text
T14: remaining time of a round on phone and dashboard (B21, Q9 a), U4 checks it on a real phone

Evidence: pytest -q 44 passed. Review: CONDITIONAL (U4).
```

The board afterwards shows the step under "Recent activity", the task is gone from "Open tasks", and `U4` waits
under "Waiting for you":

```text
## Recent activity
- 2026-03-12 — T14: remaining time of a round on phone and dashboard (Q9 a), U4 open
```

The next session, with no memory of this one, reads the board, the journal and the open entries and goes on from
there. That is the point of keeping state in files.

## What to take from it

- You said one sentence, answered one question and, at most, did one check on a phone. The rest was the
  assistant's work, in steps you could read afterwards.
- Every step left a file: backlog item, concept, question, task, journal entry, commit. Nothing lived only in
  the chat.
- Each step has a rule behind it, so you can switch it off or change it: [Layers and overrides](/agentic-coding-template-docs/concepts/layers/).
