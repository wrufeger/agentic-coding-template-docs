Setting it up takes one sentence in the chat. From then on every session picks up where the last one stopped.

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="Skills and sub-agents" icon="puzzle">
    More than twenty skills for everyday work, from idea to task, bug fix, refactoring, and release, and
    nine worker roles, each with its own model tier. See [Skills](/agentic-coding-template-docs/reference/skills/)
    and [Roles](/agentic-coding-template-docs/reference/roles/).
  </Card>
  <Card title="Rules that are followed" icon="approve-check">
    Concept before code, "done" only with evidence, nothing irreversible without your yes, no secrets in
    commands, commits, or logs. In Claude Code, hooks enforce the mechanical ones. See
    [Rules](/agentic-coding-template-docs/reference/rules/).
  </Card>
  <Card title="A record of the work" icon="open-book">
    Tasks, backlog, questions, and a journal live in `docs/ai/`, versioned with your code, so a fresh
    session or a new team member can continue without a hand-over meeting.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Yours to change, safe to update" icon="setting">
    One file, `docs/ai/config.md`, steers the workflow. Every rule can be switched off or replaced, and
    `act-update` brings in a newer template without overwriting your own content. See
    [Layers and overrides](/agentic-coding-template-docs/concepts/layers/).
  </Card>
</CardGrid>
